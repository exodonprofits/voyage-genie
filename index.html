Steps 2 & 3 · Trip cost tracker

Group money, handled the way real trips work

Not every cost is split evenly, and not every traveler pays for themselves. The tracker handles the messy parts.

  • 👨‍👩‍👧
    Families count as one partyA couple or a family of four can share one balance.
  • 🎯
    Split with just the people involvedOnly three went on the boat? Only three pay for it.
  • 💝
    Cover someone elseParents paying for the kids, or a birthday trip on the group.
  • ✅
    Fewest payments, marked paidRecord who's paid back and how, so nobody's chasing anyone.
  • 🔗
    Share it, export itA read-only link for the group, plus CSV and print.
