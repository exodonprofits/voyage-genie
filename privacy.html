Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026 · Voyage Genie is operated by Exodon Profits LLC, Illinois, USA

The short version

  • We collect what you type in (trip plans, expenses, the people on your trips) and your account details, to run the site.
  • No advertising, no analytics trackers, and we don't sell or rent your data.
  • AI features send your trip details to an AI model provider to write your plan.
  • Partner links (tours, hotels, flights, insurance) may earn us a commission. The partner sees your visit once you click.
  • You can delete your Voyage Genie data yourself on the Account page, or email support@voyagegenie.com.

1. What we collect

Account

Your email address, the name you give us, and a password (stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider). If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us. Your sign-in is shared across apps in the GenieSphere family run by Exodon Profits LLC.

Trip planning

What you enter in the trip planners, the Smart Trip Finder and the Genie chat: destinations, dates, budget, group size, interests and preferences. If you ask us to email your itinerary, the email address you give for that.

Cost tracker and group trips

Trip names and dates, expenses (amount, date, description, who paid and who shares it), payments between members, and the people on the trip: their names, party sizes and, if you add them, their email and payment handles (for example a Venmo or Zelle name). Only add people who are fine with being on the trip.

Other

  • Saved plans and your recent planner searches, so you can return to them.
  • Messages you send through the contact form.
  • Basic technical data our hosting and service providers log automatically, such as IP address, browser type and the time of a request.

2. How we use it

  • To run the features you use: generate plans, save trips, calculate who owes whom, send the itinerary emails you ask for.
  • To keep the service secure and working, and to answer your messages.
  • To improve the site, using the content above. We don't build advertising profiles.

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

4. Partner links and affiliates

Some links send you to travel companies such as Viator, GetYourGuide, Booking.com, Hotels.com, Expedia, Skyscanner, SafetyWing, World Nomads, Airalo, Holafly, Priority Pass and Kiwitaxi. When you click one, that company receives your visit and may set its own cookies to credit us with a commission. What happens on their site is governed by their privacy policy. See the affiliate disclosure.

5. Sharing within a trip

People on the same trip can see that trip's members, expenses and payments. If a trip owner creates a read-only share link, anyone with the link can see the trip's names, expenses and balances, but not members' emails or payment handles. Share links can expire, and the owner can stop sharing at any time by deleting the trip or asking us.

6. Cookies and browser storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The site uses your browser's local storage to keep you signed in, remember your last plan and form inputs, and keep a short history of your recent searches on that device. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings. Partner sites you visit through our links may set their own cookies.

7. How long we keep it

We keep your data while you use Voyage Genie, until you delete it or ask us to. Contact messages are kept as long as needed to answer them, and deleted on request. Logs kept by hosting and service providers are kept for their usual periods.

8. Your choices: export and deletion

  • Export: the Account page downloads your saved plans as a file. For a copy of everything else, email us.
  • Delete: "Delete My Voyage Genie Data" on the Account page removes your saved plans, search history, profile, and every trip you created (with its expenses, payments and share links). On trips other people created, your name stays, because it is part of their records and balances, but your account, email and payment handles are removed from it.
  • Your sign-in: it is shared with other GenieSphere apps, so deleting your Voyage Genie data doesn't delete it. To delete the sign-in itself, email us.
  • Someone else added you to a trip? Ask them to remove you, or email us and we'll help.

You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information by emailing support@voyagegenie.com. We respond within 30 days and honor these requests wherever you live. We may need to confirm the request comes from the account's email address.

9. Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Database access is limited by per-user rules, so other users can't read your trips unless you are on the same trip or have shared a link. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Children

Voyage Genie is not meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has given us information, email us and we'll delete it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes we'll also tell signed-in users on the site.
