💝 Covering Someone Else's Share

Use this when someone absorbs another person's costs — a parent paying for their kids, or one person treating the group. The covered person's balance shrinks and the sponsor's grows.

Not to be confused with: Assign Payers (Step 1), which only says who physically hands over the cash for someone — it doesn't move any money between them; and 👪 Group families (Step 3), which just merges rows together on screen and changes no maths at all. Only this panel actually shifts what someone owes.
